Know before you buy

The essentials

Use these practical checks to avoid buying something that looks good but doesn’t fit your needs.

🎮 Controllers

Choose wired USB for simple setup or wireless when freedom matters. Confirm PC compatibility, connection mode, button layout and whether the game supports controllers.

🖱️ Gaming mice

Compare comfort, sensor performance, weight, side buttons and wired/wireless connection. More DPI does not automatically mean a better mouse.

⌨️ Keyboards

Choose layout first (full-size or compact), then consider switch feel, anti-ghosting, key rollover, build quality and whether the layout suits your work too.

🎧 Headsets

Look for comfort, mic clarity, connection type and return policy. Check whether it uses a 3.5 mm jack, USB, Bluetooth or a dedicated wireless receiver.

✅ Quick checklist

Before you hit “Buy”

  • Confirm Windows/PC compatibility and supported connection type.
  • Check dimensions, layout, included receiver/cable and software needs.
  • Read recent customer reviews, especially repeated complaints.
  • Verify seller, warranty, returns and current price on the listing.
